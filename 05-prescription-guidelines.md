# Prescription Guidelines: Specific, Actionable Interventions

*Based on clinical trial protocols and guideline recommendations*

---

## Overview: The Multi-Modal Prescription

| Domain | Target | Evidence Basis |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Cholesterol | LDL <70 mg/dL | AHA/ASA Guidelines; SPARCL, TST trials |
| Exercise | 150 min/week moderate aerobic | WHO Guidelines; Meta-analyses |
| Fiber | 25-30g/day total; 5-10g soluble | Meta-analyses; FDA recommendations |
| Blood Pressure | <130/80 mmHg | SPRINT-MIND |

---

## Prescription 1: Cholesterol Management

### Target

| Metric | Goal | Rationale |
|--------|------|-----------|
| LDL-C | **<70 mg/dL** | Secondary stroke prevention standard |
| Percent reduction | **≥50%** | High-intensity statin effect |
| Lower acceptable | 55-70 mg/dL | Safe; 18-26% additional benefit |
| Avoid | <30 mg/dL | Limited additional benefit |

### Medication Protocol

#### Step 1: High-Intensity Statin

| Option | Dose | Expected LDL Reduction |
|--------|------|----------------------|
| Atorvastatin | **80 mg daily** | 50-55% |
| Rosuvastatin | **40 mg daily** | 50-55% |
| Alternative: Atorvastatin | 40 mg daily | 40-50% |

*Note: SPARCL trial used atorvastatin 80mg*

#### Step 2: If LDL Still >70 mg/dL

| Addition | Dose | Additional Reduction |
|----------|------|---------------------|
| **Ezetimibe** | 10 mg daily | 15-20% |

#### Step 3: If Still Above Goal (Very High Risk)

| Addition | Dose | Additional Reduction |
|----------|------|---------------------|
| Evolocumab (Repatha) | 140 mg SC every 2 weeks | 50-60% |
| OR Alirocumab (Praluent) | 75-150 mg SC every 2 weeks | 50-60% |

### Monitoring Schedule

| Timepoint | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Baseline | Fasting lipid panel, LFTs |
| 4-6 weeks | Repeat lipids; assess tolerability |
| 3 months | Lipids if dose adjusted; LFTs if indicated |
| Every 6-12 months | Maintenance monitoring |

### Safety Monitoring

| Concern | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Myalgia (muscle pain) | Check CK; consider switching statins |
| LFTs >3x ULN | Hold statin; recheck; may restart at lower dose |
| Memory complaints | Reassess; evidence shows no causal link |

## Prescription 2: Exercise Program

### Weekly Structure

| Day | Activity | Duration | Intensity |
|-----|----------|----------|-----------|
| Monday | Aerobic (walking) | 30-45 min | Moderate |
| Tuesday | Strength + Balance | 30-45 min | Light-Moderate |
| Wednesday | Aerobic (cycling/walking) | 30-45 min | Moderate |
| Thursday | Rest or light stretching | 20 min | Light |
| Friday | Combined aerobic + strength | 45-60 min | Moderate |
| Saturday | Aerobic (walking outdoors) | 30-45 min | Moderate |
| Sunday | Balance/Tai Chi | 20-30 min | Light |

**Weekly Total**: ~150-180 minutes aerobic + 2-3 strength sessions

### Heart Rate Targets

#### Calculation Method (Karvonen Formula)

```
Target HR = (Max HR - Resting HR) × Intensity% + Resting HR
Max HR = 220 - Age
```

#### For a 75-Year-Old

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Estimated Max HR | 145 bpm (220 - 75) |
| Assume Resting HR | 70 bpm |
| Heart Rate Reserve | 75 bpm (145 - 70) |

| Intensity Level | % HRR | Target HR Range |
|-----------------|-------|-----------------|
| Light (warm-up) | 40-50% | 100-108 bpm |
| **Moderate (goal)** | **50-70%** | **108-123 bpm** |
| Vigorous (if cleared) | 70-85% | 123-134 bpm |

**Simplified Target**: **100-120 bpm** during exercise

#### If on Beta-Blocker

Beta-blockers reduce maximum heart rate. Use:
- **RPE (Rating of Perceived Exertion)**: Target 12-14 on 6-20 scale
- **Talk Test**: Can speak short sentences but not sing

### Exercise Progression

#### Phase 1: Weeks 1-4 (Acclimatization)

| Parameter | Prescription |
|-----------|-------------|
| Frequency | 3 days/week |
| Duration | 20-30 minutes |
| Intensity | 50-60% HRR (100-115 bpm) OR RPE 11-12 |
| Type | Walking on flat ground |
| Supervision | Required |

#### Phase 2: Weeks 5-12 (Building)

| Parameter | Prescription |
|-----------|-------------|
| Frequency | 4 days/week |
| Duration | 35-45 minutes |
| Intensity | 60-70% HRR (115-123 bpm) OR RPE 12-14 |
| Type | Walking + stationary bike |
| Add | Resistance training 2x/week |

#### Phase 3: Week 13+ (Maintenance)

| Parameter | Prescription |
|-----------|-------------|
| Frequency | 5 days/week |
| Duration | 45-60 minutes |
| Intensity | 60-70% HRR OR RPE 12-14 |
| Type | Varied (walking, cycling, swimming if able) |
| Continue | Resistance 2x/week; Balance 2-3x/week |

### Strength Training Protocol

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Sit-to-stand | 2 × 10 | From chair, no hands if possible |
| Wall push-ups | 2 × 10 | Progress to counter, then floor |
| Seated leg lifts | 2 × 10 each leg | With ankle weight if tolerated |
| Arm curls | 2 × 10 each arm | 2-5 lb weights |
| Standing heel raises | 2 × 10 | Hold chair for balance |
| Seated row (band) | 2 × 10 | Resistance band anchored |

**Progression**: When 10 reps become easy, add 1-2 lbs or increase resistance

### Balance Training Protocol

| Exercise | Duration | Frequency |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| Single-leg stance | 30 sec each leg | Daily |
| Heel-to-toe walking | 10 steps | 3x/week |
| Side stepping | 10 steps each direction | 3x/week |
| Tai Chi or similar | 20-30 min | 2x/week |

### Safety Requirements

| Requirement | Implementation |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Supervision** | Always have someone present |
| **Environment** | Clear of obstacles; non-slip surfaces |
| **Footwear** | Supportive, non-slip shoes |
| **Hydration** | Water before, during, after |
| **Stop signs** | Chest pain, severe breathlessness, dizziness → stop immediately |
| **Medical clearance** | Physician approval before starting |

## Prescription 3: Dietary Fiber

### Daily Targets

| Fiber Type | Target | Primary Function |
|------------|--------|------------------|
| Total fiber | **25-30 g/day** | General health |
| Soluble fiber | **5-10 g/day** | LDL reduction |

### Daily Meal Plan Template

#### Breakfast (~8-10g fiber)

| Food | Amount | Fiber |
|------|--------|-------|
| Oatmeal (cooked) | 1.5 cups | 4g |
| Berries | 1 cup | 3g |
| Ground flaxseed | 1 tbsp | 2g |
| **Subtotal** | | **9g** |

#### Lunch (~8-10g fiber)

| Food | Amount | Fiber |
|------|--------|-------|
| Bean soup or salad | 1 cup beans | 4g |
| Whole grain bread | 2 slices | 4g |
| Vegetables | 1 cup | 3g |
| **Subtotal** | | **11g** |

#### Dinner (~6-8g fiber)

| Food | Amount | Fiber |
|------|--------|-------|
| Sweet potato | 1 medium | 4g |
| Broccoli | 1 cup | 2.5g |
| Brown rice | 1/2 cup | 2g |
| **Subtotal** | | **8.5g** |

#### Snacks (~3-5g fiber)

| Food | Amount | Fiber |
|------|--------|-------|
| Apple | 1 medium | 2.5g |
| Almonds | 1 oz | 1.5g |
| **Subtotal** | | **4g** |

**Daily Total**: ~32g fiber

| Food | Serving | Soluble Fiber |
|------|---------|---------------|
| Psyllium husk (Metamucil) | 1 tbsp | 5g |
| Oat bran | 1/2 cup | 3g |
| Oatmeal (cooked) | 1 cup | 2g |
| Black beans | 1/2 cup | 2.4g |
| Kidney beans | 1/2 cup | 2g |

| Pear | 1 medium | 1.5g |

| Orange | 1 medium | 1.5g |
| Brussels sprouts | 1 cup | 2g |
| Sweet potato | 1 medium | 1.5g |
| Ground flaxseed | 1 tbsp | 1.1g |

### Psyllium Supplementation Protocol

**If dietary fiber is insufficient, add:**

| Timing | Dose | Instructions |
|--------|------|--------------|
| Before breakfast | 1 tbsp psyllium in 8 oz water | Drink immediately; follow with more water |
| Before dinner | 1 tbsp psyllium in 8 oz water | Same |

**Important**:
- Start with 1 tsp and increase gradually over 1-2 weeks
- Must drink adequate water (8-10 cups/day)
- Take medications 1 hour before or 2-4 hours after psyllium

## Prescription 4: Blood Pressure Management

### Target

| Parameter | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Systolic BP | **<130 mmHg** |
| Diastolic BP | **<80 mmHg** |
| Alternative if frail | <140/90 mmHg |

*Based on SPRINT-MIND: Intensive BP control showed 19% reduction in MCI*

### Monitoring

| Frequency | Method |
|-----------|--------|
| Weekly | Home BP monitoring |
| Each physician visit | Office measurement |

**Watch for**: Orthostatic hypotension (dizziness on standing) - increases fall risk

## Monitoring and Follow-up Schedule

| Timepoint | Tests/Assessments |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Baseline** | MMSE/MoCA, lipid panel, BP, weight |
| **Week 4-6** | Lipid panel (statin response) |
| **Month 3** | MMSE/MoCA, BP, weight, exercise log review |
| **Month 6** | Lipid panel, MMSE/MoCA, functional assessment |
| **Month 12** | Complete reassessment |

### Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| LDL-C | <70 mg/dL maintained |
| Exercise adherence | ≥80% of prescribed sessions |
| MMSE decline | <2 points/year (vs expected 3-4) |
| ADL maintenance | Stable or improved |
| Falls | Reduced or zero |

---

## When to Contact Healthcare Provider

| Concern | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Muscle pain (severe) | Contact within 24-48 hours |
| Chest pain during exercise | Stop immediately; call if persists |
| Falls | Report at next visit; may need PT eval |
| Sudden confusion change | Same day contact |
| New weakness one side | 911 - possible stroke |

---

*These prescriptions should be reviewed and approved by the patient's healthcare team before implementation.*
