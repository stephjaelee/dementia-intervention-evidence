t# Executive Summary: What Helps and What to Do

*Written in plain language for family members*

---

## The Bottom Line

**Exercise and cholesterol control are as effective as expensive medications** for slowing memory loss. They work through different pathways and can be combined with medications for the best results.

---

## What the Research Shows

### Exercise Works

| Finding | What It Means |
|---------|---------------|
| Exercise matches medication effectiveness | Walking and moving helps the brain as much as memory pills |
| 150 minutes per week is the target | About 30 minutes, 5 days a week |
| Benefits appear in 3-6 months | It takes time, but improvements are measurable |
| Keeps body and mind working longer | Helps with daily activities like dressing and eating |

### Lowering Cholesterol Prevents More Damage

| Finding | What It Means |
|---------|---------------|
| LDL cholesterol should be below 70 | "Bad cholesterol" needs to be low to protect the brain |
| Statins reduce stroke risk by 22-28% | Each prevented stroke saves brain function |
| Fiber helps lower cholesterol naturally | Oatmeal, beans, and fruits add to medication effects |

---

## What This Does NOT Mean

**We cannot "cure" or "reverse" the damage already done.**

What we CAN do:
- **Slow down** how fast memory gets worse
- **Prevent** new strokes that would cause sudden drops
- **Keep the body strong** so daily life stays manageable longer

## The Simple Prescription

### Daily Exercise Target

| Activity | How Much | How Often |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| Walking (brisk) | 30-45 minutes | 5 days/week |
| Heart rate goal | 87-102 beats per minute | During exercise |
| Strength exercises | 15-20 minutes | 2 days/week |

**The "Talk Test"**: During exercise, you should be able to talk but not sing. If you can sing, go faster. If you can't talk, slow down.

### Daily Food Targets

| Food | Amount | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| Fiber (total) | 25-30 grams/day | Lowers cholesterol |
| Oatmeal | 1 cup cooked | 3g fiber, lowers LDL |
| Beans | 1 cup cooked | 4g fiber, lowers LDL |
| Berries | 1 cup | Good for brain |
| Green vegetables | 1-2 servings | Protects brain cells |

### Medication Target

| Test | Target Number | Why |
|------|---------------|-----|
| LDL Cholesterol | Below 70 mg/dL | Prevents strokes |
| Blood Pressure | Below 130/80 | Protects blood vessels |

## Why Exercise Works (Simple Explanation)

Think of the brain like a garden:
- **Exercise is like fertilizer** - it helps brain cells grow and stay healthy
- **Cholesterol control is like keeping the pipes clean** - blood flows better to feed the brain
- **Medications are like pest control** - they help manage specific problems

**You need all three for the healthiest garden.**

## Questions Family Often Asks

### "Isn't exercise dangerous for someone with memory problems?"

**No, when supervised.** Falls are a concern, so someone should always be present. But exercise actually *reduces* fall risk by improving balance and strength.

### "Won't very low cholesterol hurt the brain?"

**No.** Large studies show no brain harm even at very low cholesterol levels. The old worry about this has been proven wrong.

### "Why not just use the new expensive medications?"

The new medications (lecanemab, donanemab):
- Cost $26,000+ per year
- Only approved for *early* stages (not mid-stage)
- Show 27-35% slowing (exercise shows 25-50%)
- Have serious side effects (brain swelling in 20-35% of patients)
- Not approved for someone with stroke history

**Exercise and cholesterol control are safer, cheaper, and often more effective.**

### "Is it too late to start?"

**No.** Even in mid-stage dementia, these interventions help. The goal changes from "prevention" to "preservation" - keeping what function remains for as long as possible.

## Action Steps for the Family

### Immediate (This Week)

1. **Talk to the doctor** about:
   - Current LDL cholesterol level
   - Whether statin dose should be increased
   - Clearance for exercise program

2. **Start simple exercise**:
   - Walk together for 15-20 minutes
   - Do it at the same time each day

### Short-term (This Month)

3. **Add more fiber to meals**:
   - Oatmeal for breakfast
   - Beans with lunch or dinner
   - Fruit for snacks

4. **Increase exercise gradually**:
   - Add 5 minutes per week
   - Goal: 30 minutes by end of month

### Long-term (Ongoing)

5. **Track progress**:
   - Note mood and energy changes
   - Keep exercise log
   - Get cholesterol checked every 3-6 months

6. **Stay consistent**:
   - Make it a daily routine
   - Exercise together as a family when possible
