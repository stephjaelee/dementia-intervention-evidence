## Mechanisms of Action

### Pharmacological Interventions

| Drug Class | Primary Mechanism | Secondary Effects | Relevance to Mixed Dementia |
|------------|-------------------|-------------------|----------------------------|
| **Cholinesterase Inhibitors** | ↑ Acetylcholine by inhibiting AChE | Mild cognitive enhancement | Moderate - addresses AD component only |
| **Memantine** | NMDA receptor antagonist | Reduces glutamate excitotoxicity | Moderate - neuroprotective, not vascular |
| **Anti-amyloid mAbs** | Clear amyloid-β plaques | Unknown downstream effects | Low - no vascular benefit; ARIA risk |
| **Statins** | HMG-CoA reductase inhibition | ↓ LDL, ↓ inflammation, ↑ cerebral blood flow | HIGH - addresses vascular component directly |

### Non-Pharmacological Interventions

| Intervention | Primary Mechanism | Secondary Effects | Relevance to Mixed Dementia |
|--------------|-------------------|-------------------|----------------------------|
| **Aerobic Exercise** | ↑ BDNF, ↑ VEGF, ↑ cerebral blood flow | ↓ Tau accumulation, ↑ hippocampal volume | HIGH - addresses both AD and vascular |
| **Resistance Training** | ↑ IGF-1, ↑ muscle mass | ↓ Falls, ↑ functional independence | MODERATE - functional benefits |
| **Cognitive Stimulation** | Neural pathway activation | ↑ Cognitive reserve | MODERATE - non-specific mechanism |
| **MIND/Mediterranean Diet** | ↓ Inflammation, antioxidants | Flavonoids may ↓ amyloid deposition | MODERATE - both AD and vascular |

## Detailed Evidence by Intervention

### 1. Cholinesterase Inhibitors in Mid-Stage Dementia

**Donepezil (Aricept)**

| Study | Population | Duration | Cognitive Outcome | Functional Outcome |
|-------|------------|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| [Cochrane Review (2018)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29923184/) | Mild-moderate AD | 24-26 weeks | ADAS-cog: -2.67 pts | ADL: +1.0 |
| [DOMINO Trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22375973/) | Moderate-severe AD | 52 weeks | SMMSE: +1.9 (vs discontinuation) | BADLS: -3.0 |
| [VaD Trials](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20558719/) | Vascular dementia | 24 weeks | ADAS-cog: -1.9-2.3 pts | NS global function |

**Key Evidence Points:**
- Effect sizes 30% smaller in VaD than pure AD
- DOMINO trial supports continuation into moderate-severe stages
- VaD trial (Román 2010): 11 deaths in donepezil group vs 0 placebo (safety signal)

**Mechanism Limitations for Mixed Dementia:**
- Does not address vascular pathology
- Does not prevent recurrent strokes
- Symptomatic only; no disease modification

---

### 2. Memantine in Moderate Dementia

**Evidence Summary ([Cochrane 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30891742/), 14 studies, ~3,700 participants)**

| Population | Cognitive Benefit | Functional Benefit | Behavioral Benefit |
|------------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Moderate-severe AD | SIB: +3.11 pts | ADL: +1.09 pts | NPI: +1.84 pts |
| Mild AD | **No significant benefit** | NS | NS |
| Vascular dementia | ADAS-cog: -2.15 pts | Uncertain | NS |

**Key Points:**
- Specifically indicated for moderate-severe AD (not mild)
- May add to ChEI benefit (DOMINO: +1.2 SMMSE)
- No synergistic effect with ChEI (additive only)
- Excellent tolerability profile

### 3. Anti-Amyloid Monoclonal Antibodies

**Lecanemab (Leqembi) - [CLARITY-AD Trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36449413/)**

| Metric | Result | Clinical Significance |
|--------|--------|----------------------|
| CDR-SB at 18 months | -0.45 pts (27% slowing) | Below MCID threshold |
| ARIA-E incidence | 12.6% | Brain edema |
| ARIA-H incidence | 17.3% | Microhemorrhages |

**Donanemab (Kisunla) - [TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37459141/)**

| Metric | Result (Low/Med Tau) | Clinical Significance |
|--------|---------------------|----------------------|
| CDR-SB at 76 weeks | -0.70 pts (36% slowing) | Marginally perceptible |
| ARIA-E incidence | 24-32% | Higher than lecanemab |
| Deaths | 3 fatal ICH | Significant safety concern |

**Why NOT Indicated for This Patient:**

1. **Stage**: FDA approval is for MCI/mild AD (MMSE 20-30), not moderate (MMSE 13-17)
2. **Vascular pathology**: Prior stroke is relative/absolute contraindication
3. **ARIA risk**: 20-35% brain edema/hemorrhage rates
4. **Mechanism mismatch**: Targets amyloid only; does not address vascular component

### 4. Statin Therapy for Vascular Protection

**Secondary Stroke Prevention Evidence**

| Trial | Population | Intervention | Outcome |
|-------|------------|--------------|---------|
| SPARCL | Post-stroke/TIA | Atorvastatin 80mg | 16% ↓ stroke; 22% ↓ MACE |
| TST (Treat Stroke to Target) | Atherosclerotic stroke | LDL <70 vs 90-110 | 22% ↓ MACE; 28% ↓ ischemic stroke |

**Cognitive Outcomes**

| Study | Finding | Limitation |
|-------|---------|------------|
| Swedish Registry (15,586 AD pts) | +0.63 MMSE points per defined daily dose at 3 years | Observational |
| Cochrane Review (Statins for AD) | No cognitive benefit in RCTs | Short duration |
| PODCAST Trial | No cognitive benefit intensive vs standard lipid lowering | Small sample |

**Clinical Interpretation:**
- Strong evidence for vascular event prevention (Grade 1A)
- Indirect cognitive benefit via preventing further brain injury
- No direct cognitive improvement in established dementia
- Continue/optimize for secondary stroke prevention regardless of cognitive effects

### 5. Aerobic Exercise

**Key Trials in Cognitive Impairment**

| Trial | Population | Intervention | Cognitive Outcome |
|-------|------------|--------------|-------------------|
| DAPA (BMJ 2018) | Mild-moderate dementia (n=494) | 4mo supervised + unsupervised | **No benefit**; ADAS-cog -1.4 pts worse |
| Liu-Ambrose (Neurology 2016) | SIVCI (n=70) | 6mo 3x/week aerobic | ADAS-cog: -1.71 pts improvement |
| ADEX (JAD 2016) | Mild AD (n=200) | 16wk 3x/week moderate-high | **No benefit** on primary outcome |

**Meta-Analyses**

| Analysis | Studies | Effect Size | Key Finding |
|----------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Cochrane (Forbes 2015) | 17 RCTs | SMD 0.43 (NS) | ADL improved; cognition NS |
| Groot (2016) | 18 RCTs | SMD 0.42 | Significant; combined aerobic+resistance best |
| 2022 Meta-analysis | 22 RCTs | MMSE +0.83 pts | Significant cognitive benefit |
| BMC Geriatrics 2019 | Multiple | SMD 1.12 | Strong effect in VCI specifically |

**Reconciling Conflicting Evidence:**
- Large pragmatic trials (DAPA, ADEX): negative
- Meta-analyses: positive
- VCI-specific trials: positive
- **Implication**: Vascular phenotype may respond better than pure AD

### 6. Cognitive Stimulation Therapy (CST)

**Evidence Summary**

| Source | Studies | Effect Size | Cost-Effectiveness |
|--------|---------|-------------|---------------------|
| Cochrane (Woods 2023) | 37 RCTs, n=2,766 | SMD 0.40 | Below NICE threshold |
| Spector (2003) original | n=201 | MMSE: +1.98 pts | £75 per MMSE point |

**Comparison to Pharmacotherapy:**
- Effect sizes overlap with ChEIs (CST: 0.35-0.58; ChEIs: 0.31-0.40)
- Cost: ~$25 per program vs $200-500/year for drugs
- Safety: No medical risks vs GI side effects with ChEIs

## Recommendations for This Patient

### Tier 1: Primary Interventions (Strong Evidence)

1. **Continue/Optimize Statin Therapy**
   - Target: LDL <70 mg/dL
   - Strongest evidence for preventing further vascular injury
   - Indirect cognitive preservation

2. **Structured Exercise Program**
   - 150 min/week moderate aerobic
   - 2x/week resistance training
   - Supervised for safety
   - Effect size comparable to medications

### Tier 2: Standard of Care (Moderate Evidence)

3. **Cholinesterase Inhibitor (if not on one)**
   - Donepezil 10mg preferred (best tolerability)
   - Modest symptomatic benefit
   - Consider continuing if already taking

4. **Memantine**
   - Specifically indicated for moderate-severe AD
   - Can add to ChEI
   - Excellent tolerability

### Tier 3: Adjunctive (Supportive Evidence)

5. **Cognitive Stimulation Therapy**
   - Effect size comparable to drugs
   - Highly cost-effective
   - No medical risks

6. **MIND Diet Implementation**
   - Emphasis on fiber for cholesterol
   - Anti-inflammatory benefits

### Not Recommended

- **Anti-amyloid therapies**: Not indicated for disease stage; contraindicated with vascular pathology
- **Intensive BP lowering without monitoring**: Falls risk in dementia
