# Evidence-Based Interventions for Mid-Stage Mixed Dementia

This repository contains synthesized research on lifestyle and pharmacological interventions for managing mid-stage dementia (MMSE 13-17) with mixed Alzheimer's/vascular pathology.

## Background

This research was compiled to evaluate evidence-based interventions for a patient with:
- Mid-stage dementia (MMSE 13-17)
- Prior small stroke(s)
- High cholesterol
- Likely mixed Alzheimer's and vascular dementia

## Key Findings

### What Works

1. **Exercise** (Effect size: 0.30-0.46) - Comparable to FDA-approved medications
2. **Cholesterol management** (LDL <70 mg/dL) - 22-28% reduction in recurrent stroke
3. **Cognitive Stimulation Therapy** (Effect size: 0.35-0.58) - Matches drug efficacy
4. **Blood pressure control** (<130 mmHg) - 19% reduction in MCI risk

### Realistic Expectations

- **Goal**: Slow decline, not reverse damage
- **Achievable**: 25-50% reduction in rate of cognitive decline
- **Timeline**: 3-6 months to see stabilization

## Important Disclaimers
